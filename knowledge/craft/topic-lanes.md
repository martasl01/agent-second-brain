# Craft — Topic Lanes

The case study describes **7 topic lanes with energy tracking**. Lanes keep the content focused on a few compounding themes instead of chasing every idea; "energy" tracks how much appetite you currently have to write in each lane, so the system can route ideas toward lanes you'll actually finish.

> **Provenance:** The *concept* (7 lanes + energy) is from the case study. The *specific lanes below are starters* — replace them with your own. The article does not publish Pawel's lanes.

---

## How to use this file

- When an idea arrives, route it to a lane. If it fits no lane, that's a signal: either it's off-strategy (park it) or you've found an 8th lane (rare — prune before you add).
- Keep this to **~7 lanes**. More than that and focus dissolves; the case study's number is a feature, not a coincidence.
- Update **Energy** as it shifts. Low-energy lanes don't get killed — they get rested. Route drafting toward high-energy lanes.

**Energy scale:** 🔥 high (write here now) · 🙂 steady · 😴 resting (don't force it)

## Lane hygiene

- Review energy during maintenance sessions (see `../system-maintenance.md`).
- If a lane sits at 😴 for 60+ days and produces nothing, consider archiving it and promoting a candidate.
- A lane that consistently produces top performers (see `posts/performance-log.md`) earns more drafting time — let the data, not the mood, settle ties.
