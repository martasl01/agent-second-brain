# Platform — X (Twitter)

Platform-specific rules, hooks, and templates for X. Load alongside `../writing-techniques.md` when drafting for X. The case study's headline metrics (5.2M impressions, 43k bookmarks, 7.2% engagement in 3 months) came from this surface — **bookmarks are the north-star signal here**, because they mark content worth returning to.

> **Provenance:** All rules below are **starters** unless tagged **[from case study]**. The article reports the *outcome* on X and that *data-experiment posts earn ~3× bookmarks* (Rule 1 of writing-techniques) — it does not publish X-specific hook rules. Confirm/kill these with `../posts/performance-log.md`.

---

## Rules (starter)

### Rule 1 — Optimize for the bookmark, not the like
Likes are cheap; bookmarks mean "I'll come back to this." Ask of every draft: *would someone save this to act on later?* If not, it's an opinion post — reframe toward the data-experiment format (writing-techniques Rule 1) **[ties to case study]**.

### Rule 2 — The first tweet is the whole pitch
Line 1 has to earn the expand/scroll-stop on its own. No "a thread 🧵" throat-clear before the value.

### Rule 3 — Threads earn their length
Each tweet in a thread carries one beat and pulls to the next. If a tweet doesn't advance the idea, cut it. Land the takeaway in the final tweet, not a CTA.

---

## Hook patterns

- "[Surprising number]. Here's how." — lead with the result.
- "I did [X] for [time]. [Counterintuitive finding]."
- "[Common belief] is wrong. [The data]."  *(negation also works well here)*
- "Most people [do X]. The ones who [Y] get [better outcome]."

## Templates

### Single-tweet data experiment (highest bookmark value)
```
[Result as a number or negation].

I [smallest experiment]. [Before] → [after].

Why: [one-sentence mechanism].
```

### Thread
```
1/ [Hook = result or tension. The whole pitch.]
2/ [Setup: the smallest version of what I did]
3/ [The work / the steps / the experiment]
4/ [The number / before→after]
5/ [Why it happened — the mechanism]
6/ [Takeaway the reader can apply. No CTA-bait.]
```

---

## Avoid
- "Thread 🧵" with no value in tweet 1 (violates Rule 2).
- Reply-bait and follow-for-follow.
- Padding a thread to hit a length — every tweet earns its place (Rule 3).
